About

I started my first business at 7 and mostly studied social sciences until 16. At 15 I ran a DeepFake awareness study with 259 participants. I got my first computer at 16, co-founded a game studio, and built a 7-person team with its own office.

I found a loophole in the Turkish high school system and completed 5 years in 3. At 17 I was a Founders Associate at Lucent, an EF-backed startup, and at 18 a Technical Founders Associate at microagi.

Alongside that, I do independent research on neuroscience and brain-computer interfaces. I'm an Emergent Ventures grantee and a Null Fellow, currently exploring new opportunities. I think I am at least an 8 on Ken Wilber.
