Just Learn

What It Is

A micro-learning app. You type any topic or multiple topics — physics, philosophy, cooking, history — and the AI generates 15-second swipeable learning cards.

How It Works

TikTok-style swipe mechanics, smart pacing for habit-building, addictive in the way that's actually good for you. It's a PWA — works without signup and lives on your home screen.
