What It Is

Two concept BCI designs — an invasive intracortical implant and a semi-invasive subdural µECoG array — rendered as full engineering-drawing packs: cross-sections, exploded views, layer stack-ups, and BOMs.

Reference architectures: Neuralink N1, Blackrock Utah Array, Columbia BISC.
